# Day 01 – Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI)

## 🔹 What is Artificial Intelligence?
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of machines to simulate human intelligence.
It allows systems to learn from data, make decisions, and improve over time.

AI enables computers to:
- Understand language
- Recognize images
- Make predictions
- Automate tasks

---

## 🔹 Why AI is Important Today?
AI is used everywhere in modern technology:

- Google search results
- Netflix recommendations
- Chatbots and virtual assistants
- Fraud detection systems

AI helps in reducing manual work and improving efficiency.

---

## 🔹 AI vs Machine Learning vs Deep Learning
- **AI:** Broad concept of intelligent machines
- **Machine Learning (ML):** Systems that learn from data
- **Deep Learning (DL):** Neural networks inspired by human brain

---

## 🔹 Simple Example
When YouTube suggests videos based on your watch history,
it is using **Machine Learning**, which is a part of AI.

---

## 🔹 AI in DevOps (AIOps)
AI helps DevOps by:
- Detecting anomalies in logs
- Predicting failures
- Automating monitoring and alerts
